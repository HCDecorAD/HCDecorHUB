## SEO URL RULES
- Vietnamese production URLs are canonical for core commercial pages.
- One intent per landing page; no doorway/duplicate pages.
- Project URLs live under /du-an/.
- Journal supports informational intent; service pages support commercial intent.
- Use descriptive H1, title, meta description, image alt, breadcrumbs and internal links.
- Do not publish claims or project facts until verified.
